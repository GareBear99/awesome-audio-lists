## Editorial and promotional SEO strategy

The editorial section should rank for legitimate discovery phrases without reading like spam. Use descriptive headings and natural language around:

- audio plugin review outlets
- free VST/AU plugin promotion
- music technology editorial contacts
- open-source JUCE plugin showcase routes
- freeware plugin roundup submissions
- independent producer/developer music software pitches

Avoid claims like "best plugin," "industry standard," "certified," or "studio adopted" unless there is public proof. Strong SEO comes from clear structure, useful submission notes, and real outbound links — not repeated keyword stuffing.
